BASS-II Experimental Comparison

A source-traceable comparison of NASA-reported PMMA rod observations. Values below are reported findings, not model predictions or universal safety limits.

Microgravity · BASS-II

Steady flame spreading

13.6% O₂

NASA reports steady spreading on a ¼-inch PMMA rod in BASS-II microgravity.

1-g comparison

Blowoff condition

16.0% O₂

NASA reports a 1-g blowoff condition for a ¼-inch PMMA rod.

Engineering interpretation: The reported difference is 2.4 percentage points of oxygen. NASA describes this as a negative oxygen margin of safety and cautions that normal-gravity flammability limits should be derated. Do not treat 13.6% as a universal ignition threshold: sustained flame propagation and ignition are different phenomena, and the compared gravity environments are not interchangeable.

What PHOENIX should preserve when comparing experiments

  • Material identity and sample geometry
  • Gravity regime and test apparatus
  • Oxygen concentration and whether it is initial or final
  • Flow configuration and measured velocity or stretch rate
  • Observed outcome: ignition, sustained spread, quenching, blowoff or verified extinction
  • Source document, test identifier and measurement uncertainty

How ranking works: PHOENIX uses a visible, rule-based relevance rubric based on investigation purpose and your selected question.
