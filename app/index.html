Engineering principles

01 · PREVENTION

Understand ignition

Evaluate material identity, geometry, ignition conditions, oxygen environment and local airflow.

02 · DETECTION

Recognize early signals

Investigate smoke, temperature, gas concentrations, optical signals and sensor response.

03 · SUPPRESSION

Verify extinction

Distinguish true extinction from flame displacement or blowoff. Consider reignition and secondary ignition.

SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY

Different materials and geometries can behave differently. PHOENIX must not apply one generic flame-spread or suppression model to all cabin materials. Unknown measurements must remain unknown. A simulated scenario is not a validated spacecraft safety assessment.
