Internal Fire Intelligence Engine

Qualitative evidence-screening demonstration. It does not calculate a validated probability of ignition, flame spread or extinction.

Material-specific rule: do not combine PMMA films, slabs and rods—or unrelated fabrics and insulation—into one generic flame model. Blowoff is not proof of extinction; verify extinction and check for reignition.

External Integrity Intelligence Engine

A structured inspection-evidence screen for external thermal-protection systems, separate from internal cabin fire analysis.

An impact does not by itself establish loss of thermal protection. Characterize location, dimensions, depth, interfaces, underlying structure and configuration-specific acceptance criteria.

From NASA evidence to engineering decision

01 · DATARetrieve original experiment, metadata, files and material description.
02 · CONTEXTCheck material, geometry, oxygen, pressure, airflow, ignition and test setup.
03 · INTERPRETCompare compatible tests; report patterns, uncertainty and missing evidence.
04 · OPTIONSPresent mitigation options, trade-offs, confidence and unresolved questions.
05 · REVIEWQualified engineers evaluate evidence and make the decision.
PHOENIX is a research and decision-support prototype. It is not NASA-certified, does not connect to spacecraft sensors, and does not make operational safety decisions.
